What it is

A map of small American food makers who will send you what they make. Bakeries, smokehouses, dairies, single-product outfits that have been doing one thing for forty years.

Why it exists

Most of the good stuff is made by people who never learned search engine optimisation. They are findable if you already know their name, and invisible if you do not, which is a bad arrangement for everyone involved. This is the list you would want if you knew the list existed.
