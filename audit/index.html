What your website is actually doing

Send me your address and I send back a written report: what is broken, what it is costing you, and what I would fix. Fifty dollars, by email, inside three business days.

I do not need your password, your hosting login, or access to anything. Everything in the report is measured from the page a customer already sees.

What gets measured

On a phoneWhether the site works on the screen most of your customers are holding. Tap targets, text size, anything running off the edge.
SpeedHow long it takes to show something useful, and what is holding it up.
Being foundWhether search engines can read the page, what it claims to be about, and whether your town appears anywhere on it.
Reaching youWhether a customer can find a phone number, an address and today's hours without hunting. For a chain, whether every location has them.
LegibilityEvery text colour measured against its background for contrast, to the WCAG 4.5:1 floor. Computed, not eyeballed.
Broken thingsDead links, missing images, pages that error, and anything the browser complains about.
TrustCertificate, mixed content, and whether the site says who runs it.

What you get

One document. Each finding says what it is, where it is, why it matters in money or customers, and what fixing it costs. Findings are ordered by what I would do first.

The fixes are priced individually and quoted separately. Take all of them, take one, or take the report to another builder — it is yours and it names no names but yours.
