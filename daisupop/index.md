## What it is

A dice popper for Discord. One roll, rendered to everyone in the voice
channel simultaneously.

## Why that matters

Every remote dice game has the same argument in it: somebody rolled
somewhere nobody else could see. A shared roll removes the one excuse, and it
turns out that is most of what a dice tool needs to do.
