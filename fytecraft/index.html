FyteCraft

A real-time strategy game where combat is the game rather than the reward for twenty minutes of economy. It is alpha, it says so on the front page, and the source is public while that is true.

Read the sourceWhat alpha means here

Alpha · free · source public · things are missing and will change

What it is

A real-time strategy game where combat is the game rather than the reward for twenty minutes of economy. Most RTS makes you spend the opening building something before anything happens; this starts at the part people stayed for.

It is early

Alpha, and said so on the front page rather than discovered on launch. Things are missing, things will change, and the source is public while that is true. If you want a finished game this is not one yet.
