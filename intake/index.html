Straight answers

Do I own the site?

Yes, once the build is paid for. The files are yours. If you leave, you take them with you.

What happens if I cancel?

Email me and it stops at the end of the month you've already paid for. No notice period, no cancellation fee, nothing to argue about. The site goes offline because hosting stops, but you keep the files and the domain — ask within 30 days and I'll hand both over for free.

Why is the build only $100?

Because the monthly is the business, and because $100 is a price you can say yes to without a meeting. I'd rather build ten sites this month than argue about one $3,000 proposal.

I already have a website and it's bad.

Same price. Replacing a bad site is usually easier than starting cold, because the words already exist somewhere.

Can you take payments on my site?

Usually yes — that's one of the add-ons. Some businesses can't, because the payment companies won't process certain products. If that's you, I'll tell you straight and build you a site that sends people to you instead.

Will you put my business on your website?

Not without asking you first. I can link to your site as an example of my work — it's public either way — but your logo and your name don't go in my advertising unless you tell me they can. That's written into the agreement, not just a promise here.

Is there a contract?

Yes, and it's on the last page of the intake form where you can read it before you agree to anything. It's month-to-month. Read it. If something in it bothers you, say so and we'll talk about it before you sign.
