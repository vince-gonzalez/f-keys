**$100 one-time · $75/month · cancel any time** · F-Keys Creative LLC (FL)

## The price

**$100 to build it.** A real site written and built for your business. Works on phones. Your photos, your words, your hours, your prices. Contact and booking that reach you. Set up so Google can find you. You see it and approve it before it goes live.

**$75/month to keep it running.** Hosting, your domain renewed and managed, two edit requests a month, one hour of Zoom or phone, and security monitoring. Month-to-month — cancel whenever.

An **edit request** is one batch of changes: send a list, I do the whole list. Two of those a month, not two individual words. Anything beyond that gets quoted before I do it, never after.

## Add-ons

There are 47 extras on the intake form — a store, online booking, a blog, another language, and forty more. Twenty of them are already included in the $75 and cost nothing. The rest are $15 or $50 one-time. Three of them are ongoing monthly work and cost $50/month. Every price is printed on the menu. Nothing is "call for pricing."

## How it goes

1. You fill out the intake form. About fifteen minutes, and it asks everything I need so I'm not calling you six times.
2. I send you a written quote with the same numbers you saw, plus the date I'll have it done. Nothing is charged.
3. You accept it in writing and I build it. You see it before anyone else.
4. It goes live when you approve it. The $75/month starts then — not while I'm still building.

## Straight answers

**Do I own the site?** Yes, once the build is paid for. The files are yours. If you leave, you take them with you.

**What happens if I cancel?** Hosting stops, so the site goes down. You keep the files and the domain. Tell me in writing and that's it.

**Why is the build only $100?** The monthly is the business, and $100 is a price you can say yes to without a meeting.

**Can you take payments on my site?** Usually — it's one of the add-ons. Some businesses can't, because the payment processors won't handle certain products. If that's you I'll say so and build you a site that sends people to you instead.

**Is there a contract?** Yes, on the last page of the intake form, where you can read it before agreeing to anything. It's month-to-month. Submitting the form does not start work and does not charge you — work begins only once you accept a written quote.
