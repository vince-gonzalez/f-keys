Key-J Guide

Every key on your keyboard plays a musical note. The current scale/octave determines which notes are mapped to which keys.

Tap a key for staccato. Hold a key for sustained notes. The app remembers every note and where you left off.

Play Styles: Staccato = short & punchy. Vibrato = pitch wobble. Tremolo = amplitude flutter. Arpeggio = chord spread. Legato = smooth crossfade.

Re-bind keys: Click any key in the left panel to change its note assignment.

Export: Exports your entire session as a WAV file. No time limit.
