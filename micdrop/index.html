MICDROP

BY D00B

GRAB THE MIC.
SURVIVE THE ROOM.

A live open-mic arena. Take the stage for 120 seconds and win the crowd, or eat three boos and get buzzed off to a sad trombone. Out in the crowd you laugh, boo, throw tomatoes, and hammer the soundboard — spam it and it's off to club jail.

Bring friends. Bring jokes. Bring chaos.

THE ROOM

120 SECONDS

That's the set. Long enough to find a rhythm, short enough that dying on stage stays funny.

THREE BOOS AND YOU'RE OFF

The crowd decides. Take three and the sad trombone plays you out, which is its own kind of applause.

THE CROWD HAS BUTTONS

Laugh, boo, throw tomatoes, work the soundboard. Watching is a role, not a waiting room.

CLUB JAIL

Hammer the soundboard hard enough and you get removed from the room. Heckling is welcome; drowning out the stage is not.

RANK UP BY DOING SETS

Stack sets and climb from Open Mic Rookie to Legend of the Mic. The only way up is more stage time.

PUBLIC OR PRIVATE

Open the room to strangers, or keep it to the people you came with.
