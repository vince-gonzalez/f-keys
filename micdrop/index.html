What it does

A live open-mic arena. Take the stage for 120 seconds and win the crowd, or eat three boos and get buzzed off to a sad trombone. Out in the crowd you laugh, boo, throw tomatoes and work the soundboard; spam it and it is off to club jail.

Stack up sets and climb from Open Mic Rookie to Legend of the Mic. Public rooms for strangers, private ones for bombing among friends.
