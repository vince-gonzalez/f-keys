## What it does

Draw notes onto a 96-row piano roll, pick a waveform per channel, set the tempo and
press play. When it sounds right, take it away as a WAV or as a ringtone.

## Controls

Draw and erase, play, stop and rewind, tempo in BPM, zoom from 1x to 8x, extend the
board and clear it. Square, saw, triangle and sine per channel.

One page, no dependencies. It opens and it works.
