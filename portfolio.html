Vince Gonzalez

Punta Gorda, Florida. Operations supervisor at Federal Express; founder of F-Keys Creative LLC. Everything below was built independently and is running now.

  • 58 deposited works, every one with a DOI and open access, all deposited in 2026. ORCID
  • Nine contributions merged into set.mm, the Metamath main library, reviewed and accepted by its maintainers. Several remove an avoidable axiom from an existing proof, which means rebuilding the proof rather than restating the theorem.
  • Twelve properties live, built end to end: the measurement, the code, the deployment and the writing.

The research below reports what the data does not support alongside what it does. Negative results are published as negative results. Selected writing quotes the source of every excerpt.
