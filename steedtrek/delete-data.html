Data & Account Deletion

Steed Trek · F-Keys Creative LLC · Effective 2 November 2026

There is nothing for us to delete. Steed Trek has no user accounts and keeps no data about you on any server. We never collect your personal information, so there is none to remove on our side.

Removing the app’s on-device data

Steed Trek stores only a local copy of its own files on your device so it can run offline. To remove that local data completely:

  • iPhone / iPad: delete the app (touch and hold its icon, then Remove App → Delete App). This removes the app and all of its local data.
  • Android: uninstall the app, or go to Settings → Apps → Steed Trek → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Web version: clear site data for the game in your browser’s settings (Site settings → Clear data), or remove it from your home screen if you installed it there.

Requesting confirmation

If you want written confirmation that we hold no data about you, email hello@f-keys.com and we will respond. Because we collect nothing, we cannot look you up by any account — there is simply nothing on file.
