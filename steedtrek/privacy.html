Privacy Policy

Steed Trek · F-Keys Creative LLC · Effective 2 November 2026

The short version: Steed Trek collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no trackers. The game runs entirely on your device and works offline. We have no servers that receive anything about you.

Who we are

Steed Trek is made and published by F-Keys Creative LLC (“we,” “us”), a limited liability company organized in the State of Florida, USA. This policy explains how the Steed Trek app and web version handle information.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store, sell, share, or transmit any personal data. We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any identifier. The game has no login and no user accounts.

On-device storage

So the game can load quickly and run without a connection, your device may keep a local copy of the app’s own files (and, on the web version, a service-worker cache). This stays on your device, is controlled by your device or browser, contains no personal information, and is never sent to us or anyone else. You can clear it at any time — see Data & Deletion.

No third-party analytics, ads, or trackers

Steed Trek includes no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no third-party tracking software of any kind.

The app store you downloaded from

If you installed Steed Trek from the Apple App Store or Google Play, that store may collect standard information (such as device type, download and purchase records, and crash diagnostics) under its own privacy policy, which we do not control. Please review the privacy policy of the store you used.

Children

Steed Trek is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children, including children under 13. We comply with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by collecting nothing.

Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA)

You keep all rights available under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation and the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the rights to access, correct, delete, port, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, delete, or opt out of. We do not sell or share personal data — we have none. If you wish to confirm this, contact us below.

Changes

If we ever change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
