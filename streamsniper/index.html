STREAM SNIPER

THEY GO LIVE.
IT'S ON THE TV.

Point a spare mini PC at the television and give it a list of Twitch channels. It shows a clock until someone goes live, then puts them fullscreen with the ads stripped out. When the stream ends, the clock comes back.

HOW IT BEHAVES

YOUR CHANNELS

One username per line in a text file. Order is priority — if two are live, the one nearer the top gets the screen.

NEVER INTERRUPTS

A stream that's already playing runs to the end. Nobody higher in the list can cut in halfway through.

NO ADS IN THE PATH

Plays through Streamlink into VLC with low latency, which keeps the pre-roll out of the way.

STANDBY CLOCK

A fullscreen clock while everything is offline, so the screen is never just a black rectangle or a desktop.

STARTS ITSELF

Installs as a systemd service and comes up on boot. Power cut, then power back, and it carries on.

EDIT AND WALK AWAY

Changing the channel list applies at the next check. No restart, no terminal.

SETTING IT UP

  1. A mini PC on Ubuntu or Debian, plugged into a display
  2. bash install.sh
  3. Type the channel names when it asks
  4. sudo systemctl start streamsniper@$USER

The installer pulls in Streamlink, VLC, and pygame, then registers the service so it starts on every boot. Change the list any time in ~/.streamsniper/channels.txt.
