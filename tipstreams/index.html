What it is

Branded tip pages and OBS overlays for streamers. A multi-payment QR router, crypto and the usual platforms, pointed at your accounts.

The only sentence that matters in this category

The widget never touches the money. It routes a viewer to your payment method and gets out of the way. Nothing is held, nothing is forwarded, there is no balance, and there is no point at which a decision of ours affects whether you get paid.

Custodial tipping products hold your money on the way through, and anything that holds it can freeze it. This one has nothing to freeze, because nothing ever arrives.
