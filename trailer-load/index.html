Trailer Load

Warehouse load training that plays like a game. Weight distribution, axle limits and stack integrity are the lesson rather than the scenery &mdash; you load a trailer, and the trailer behaves like a trailer.

Play it freeLicensing for institutions

Free to play · runs in the browser · no download · licensed separately for institutional training

What it is

A loading simulator where weight distribution, axle limits and stack integrity are the lesson rather than the scenery. You load a trailer, and the trailer behaves like a trailer.

Who it is for

Anyone who has to teach loading and would rather the first mistake happened on a screen. The cheapest way to learn what a badly distributed load does is to crush one nobody paid for.

Cost

Free to play. Licensed separately to institutions that want it for training, because a training department wants an invoice, a contact and something that will still be there next year — and that is a different arrangement from a free browser game.

Play it Licensing

How it works

  1. Open it and load a trailerIn the browser. No download and no account to play.
  2. Get the distribution wrongThat is the lesson. The cheapest way to learn what a badly distributed load does is to crush one nobody paid for.
  3. Do it again until it is boringWhich is exactly what you want from the person loading a real one.

What it teaches

Weight distributionWhere the mass sits, and what that does to the vehicle.
Axle limitsThe number that turns a full trailer into an illegal one.
Stack integrityWhat holds and what shifts, and the difference between them.
Real load theorySupport, balance, hazmat, and the veteran tricks the docks never put in a manual.
Free forever for a personNo login to play. The training is not behind a gate.
Institutional licensingA training department wants an invoice, a contact and something that will still be there next year — a different arrangement from a free browser game. Ask.
