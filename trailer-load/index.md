## What it is

A loading simulator where weight distribution, axle limits and stack
integrity are the lesson rather than the scenery. You load a trailer, and the
trailer behaves like a trailer.

## Who it is for

Anyone who has to teach loading and would rather the first mistake happened
on a screen. The cheapest way to learn what a badly distributed load does is to
crush one nobody paid for.

## Cost

Free to play. Licensed separately to institutions that want it for training,
because a training department wants an invoice, a contact and something that
will still be there next year — and that is a different arrangement from
a free browser game.
